Privacy Policy — Reveal Blast

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Reveal Blast ("the app", "we") is a block puzzle game. We built it to respect your attention and your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens with your data.

The short version

We do not ask for your name, email, or an account. Your game progress is saved only on your device. We show a small number of ads and offer a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. That's it.

What we store

Your game progress, settings, and purchases are stored locally on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Ads

To keep the game free, we show ads through AppLovin MAX and its advertising partners. To serve and measure ads, these partners may process device information such as an advertising identifier, approximate location, and app-usage data. On first launch we ask your permission (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before any tracking — you can decline, and the game plays exactly the same. Buying "Remove Ads" turns ads off entirely. See AppLovin's privacy policy at applovin.com/privacy.

Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

Reveal Blast is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can decline ad tracking when asked, reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security, or buy "Remove Ads" to stop ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
