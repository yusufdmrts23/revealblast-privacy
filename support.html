Common questions

How is my progress saved?
Your progress, streaks, and collection are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them. (Cloud sync between devices isn't available yet.)

Do I need an account or internet?
No account, ever. The game plays fully offline.

How do I turn sound, music, or haptics off?
Open the Settings tab inside the app and toggle whatever you like.

I found a bug or have an idea.
Please email me — feedback genuinely shapes what gets built next.
